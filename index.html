1What is Rollable Labs?

A studio that invents tools built on generative AI and machine learning. Instead of fixed software with fixed screens, each tool has a flexible interface that adapts to an organisation’s processes and goals, and can be shaped step by step toward a solution.

2How is it different from other AI tools?
On premise
The tools run inside your organisation, on your own infrastructure. No data leaves it.
Measured, not guessed
Generative AI makes the interface flexible; machine learning measures what is actually true in your data. That makes every solution reliable and measurable.
3What is running now?
  • Sales — open beta
  • Manufacturing — pilot
  • Maintenance — research
4How does a lab work?

We start with one interesting problem and the team that has it. We build the smallest tool that can explore it, map the processes and goals around it, and shape the tool to fit them. What works continues. What does not is stopped.

5Who is it for?

Organisations whose processes and goals do not fit standard software, and who want tools that adapt to them rather than the other way round.
