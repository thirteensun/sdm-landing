02 — How a lab works

Small on purpose. Real from day one.

A lab starts with one problem and one group of people who have it, and stays small until it has earned the right to grow.

01

Pick one problem

One costly, specific problem that a real team has today, not a market or a feature list.

02

Build the smallest tool

A working product with its own login and data, focused on that one job and nothing else.

03

Prove it on real work

Used by the people with the problem. The ones that prove themselves grow up; the rest teach us what to build next.

We'd rather ship one tool that a team relies on than ten that look good in a demo. So every lab is measured by one thing: whether the work gets easier.
