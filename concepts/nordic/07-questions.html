Rollable Labs builds focused software, one problem at a time.

1What is Rollable Labs?

A small software studio. Instead of one large platform, we build separate tools, each for one specific problem a real team has.

2How does a lab work?

We pick the problem with the team that has it, build the smallest tool that solves it, and use it on their real work. What works continues. What does not is stopped.

3What is running now?
  • Sales — open beta
  • Manufacturing — pilot
  • Maintenance — research
4Who is it for?

Teams with a costly, specific problem in their daily work, who are willing to try a new tool and tell us honestly whether it helps.

5How do we start?

Write to hello@rollable.app and describe the problem in a few sentences.
