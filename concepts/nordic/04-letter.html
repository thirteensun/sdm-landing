Hello,

Rollable Labs is a small software studio. We do not try to build a platform for everything. We pick one problem that a real team has today, build the smallest tool that solves it, and use it with that team on their real work.

If the tool helps, it grows and opens to more teams. If it does not, we stop and keep what we learned. Right now three labs are running: one in sales, one in manufacturing and one in maintenance.

If your team has a problem that costs time every week, write to us. We read every message.
